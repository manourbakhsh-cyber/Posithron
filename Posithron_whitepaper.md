## 2. The Dual-Slit Consensus Mechanism (PoUO)
The core of Posithron is the Dual-Slit model, where every node operates in two parallel dimensions:

1.  **Slit A (AI Utility):** Nodes execute requested AI workloads (inference, fine-tuning) utilizing zkML (Zero-Knowledge Machine Learning) or TEEs to verify the computation without revealing the model.
2.  **Slit B (Renewable Energy):** Nodes contribute renewable energy (solar, wind) or provide battery flexibility to the grid, verified via ZKP smart meters.

Before the QRNG beacon is released, nodes commit to their work. Once the quantum random seed is generated, the network collapses into a single reality, selecting the winning block with absolute unpredictability.
